The idea

AI calls people with debt. Applies pressure. Gets them to pay.

Debt Engine is an AI debt collection agency. The AI calls people who have debt obligations and applies pressure tactics — calling often, applying pressure, trying to get them to pay anything they can. It's offered to banks and finance companies as a service. You can also buy the debt from them for cheaper and try to get it paid back yourself. Or do it on a percentage basis — we take a cut of what we collect, and the original contractor gets the rest.

How it works

AI calls. Applies pressure. Collects what it can.

The collection process — AI-driven

Step 1 — Identify
AI has the debt list — provided by the bank or finance company, or bought at discount
Step 2 — Call
AI calls the debtor — often, repeatedly, applying pressure tactics designed to get a response
Step 3 — Collect
AI gets them to pay — anything they can. The goal is to collect as much as possible, not to judge how they pay
B2B Service

Offered to banks and finance companies

Debt Engine is offered to banks and finance companies as a collection service. They give us the debt list, we run the AI collection campaign, and we report back on what we collected. See what banks offer this service to do debt collection — or offer our service to them instead. Go to finance companies specifically and offer these services over.

Debt Purchase

Buy the debt. Collect it yourself.

Another model: we buy the debt from the bank or finance company for cheaper than face value, then try to get it paid back. If we buy a $10,000 debt for $3,000 and collect $6,000, we keep the $3,000 profit. Or do it on a percentage basis — we take a cut of what we collect, the original contractor gets the rest.

Service models

Three ways to do debt collection

Model 01

Service contract — we collect for you

The bank or finance company gives us the debt list. We run the AI collection campaign. We report on what we collected. They pay us a fee for the service — either a flat rate or a percentage of what we collect.

Best for: banks that want collection done but don't want to buy the debt.
Model 02

Debt purchase — buy it cheap, collect it

We buy the debt from the bank for cheaper than face value. Then we run the AI collection campaign and keep what we collect. Buy a $10,000 debt for $3,000, collect $6,000, keep the $3,000 difference. The more we collect, the more we make.

Best for: when the debt is likely collectable and the discount is deep enough.
Model 03

Percentage split — we collect, you split

We run the AI collection campaign on the original contractor's debt. We take a percentage of what we collect. The original contractor gets the rest. No debt purchase needed — we just collect on their behalf and split the take.

Best for: when the original contractor wants to keep ownership of the debt.

The AI

AI calls often. Applies pressure. Adapts.

The AI is the whole product. It calls people with debt obligations and applies pressure tactics — calling often, varying the approach, trying different angles to get them to pay. It's designed to get them to pay anything they can. The AI doesn't sleep, doesn't take holidays, doesn't give up. It calls as often as needed and applies as much pressure as the contract allows.

Pressure tactics

The AI applies pressure — calling often, varying the script, creating urgency, applying psychological pressure designed to get the debtor to pay. The tactics are designed to be effective, not comfortable. The goal is collection, not pleasant conversation.

Adaptable

The AI adapts to the debtor's responses. If one approach doesn't work, it tries another. It learns what works on different types of debtors and adjusts the pressure tactics accordingly. The more it runs, the better it gets at collecting.
