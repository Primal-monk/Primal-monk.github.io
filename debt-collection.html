How It Works

AI calls. Applies pressure. Collects what it can.

The Collection Process

Three steps, driven by AI.

The AI has the debt list — provided by the bank or bought at discount. It calls the debtor, often and repeatedly, applying pressure tactics designed to get a response. Then it gets them to pay anything they can. The goal is to collect as much as possible, not to judge how they pay.

Two Approaches

Service or purchase.

As a service: banks give us the debt list, we run the AI campaign, and report back on what we collected. As a purchase: we buy the debt below face value, run the campaign, and keep what we collect. Buy a $10,000 debt for $3,000, collect $6,000, keep the $3,000 difference.

Service Models

Three ways to do debt collection.

Model 01

Service contract — we collect for you

The bank or finance company gives us the debt list. We run the AI collection campaign. We report on what we collected. They pay us a fee for the service — either a flat rate or a percentage of what we collect.

Best for: banks that want collection done but don't want to buy the debt.
Model 02

Debt purchase — buy it cheap, collect it

We buy the debt from the bank for cheaper than face value. Then we run the AI collection campaign and keep what we collect. Buy a $10,000 debt for $3,000, collect $6,000, keep the $3,000 difference. The more we collect, the more we make.

Best for: when the debt is likely collectable and the discount is deep enough.
Model 03

Percentage split — we collect, you split

We run the AI collection campaign on the original contractor's debt. We take a percentage of what we collect. The original contractor gets the rest. No debt purchase needed — we just collect on their behalf and split the take.

Best for: when the original contractor wants to keep ownership of the debt.

The AI

AI calls often. Applies pressure. Adapts.

Capability

Pressure tactics

The AI applies pressure — calling often, varying the script, creating urgency, applying psychological pressure designed to get the debtor to pay. The tactics are designed to be effective, not comfortable. The goal is collection, not pleasant conversation.

Always-on · Adaptive
Capability

Adaptable by design

The AI adapts to the debtor's responses. If one approach doesn't work, it tries another. It learns what works on different types of debtors and adjusts the pressure tactics accordingly. The more it runs, the better it gets at collecting.
