TWEETSHIELD

See what you choose to see.
Concept draft — not built yet

What it does

X/Twitter already tags where a post comes from. TweetShield is a browser extension that lets you filter your feed by that country tag — either an allow-list (only show me posts from these countries) or a block-list (hide posts from these countries, show everything else).

Set it to Italy only, and that’s all you see. Block the US, China and Russia, and everything else stays. Whatever mix you want — the choice belongs to you, not to the algorithm.

This is a working mock-up of the idea, not a real filter — it does not touch your actual X/Twitter feed. Every country below is real; the filtering behind it is not.

Why it’s a browser extension, not an app

Works on the site you already use

No new app, no new account — it filters what you already see on X/Twitter itself.

Two modes, not one

Allow-list to narrow to specific countries, or block-list to exclude a few and keep everything else.

Stacks freely

Add or remove countries any time — nothing locked to a single fixed list.
