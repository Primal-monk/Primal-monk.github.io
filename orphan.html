The idea

Finance for businesses banks won't touch.

ORPHANIM is an interest-free bank for businesses — built for companies that can't get traditional financing. We lend to businesses that banks reject: startups, small businesses, companies with unconventional models, international businesses, anyone shut out of the traditional banking system. Our model: 3% equity, 0% interest. We don't profit from interest — we profit from AI trading on the spread between what's lent and what's collected. The business keeps its cash flow. We take a small piece of ownership.

How lending works

The deal: 3% equity. 0% interest.

The Deal

3% equity. 0% interest. AI trading on the spread.

ORPHANIM lends to a business. The business gets the cash it needs. No interest payments. The business keeps its cash flow. In exchange, ORPHANIM takes 3% equity in the business. That's the deal. The 3% is enough to make the lending worth it — ORPHANIM can trade on the spread and profit from market movement — but not enough to control the business. The business owner keeps 97%.

Why 3%

Enough to make it worth it. Not predatory.

3% equity is the sweet spot. It's enough to make the lending sustainable — ORPHANIM can profit from AI trading on the spread, and the 3% equity gives upside if the business succeeds. It's not predatory — the business isn't crushed by interest payments it can't afford. It keeps its cash flow. It keeps control. It just gives up 3%.

AI trading

The profit doesn't come from interest.

The Engine

Automated trading on the spread.

AI trading strategies run on the spread between what's lent and what's collected. The AI watches the market, makes trades, generates returns from market movement — not from charging interest to businesses. This is what makes 0% interest possible: the profit comes from trading, not from the borrower.

Why It Matters

0% interest is possible because of trading.

Traditional lenders charge interest because that's how they make money. ORPHANIM doesn't need to — the AI trading engine generates returns from the spread. The business gets the cash it needs without interest payments. ORPHANIM makes its money from trading, plus the 3% equity upside. Everyone wins.

Debt collection — internal

ORPHANIM collects its own debts.

When a business can't pay what it owes ORPHANIM, the same AI that powers Ophanim Financials (the AI debt collection agency offered to banks) collects ORPHANIM's own bad debt. This is internal — it's about collecting on ORPHANIM's own book, not offering a service to third parties. The AI, the pressure tactics, the collection process — same engine, different application. This keeps ORPHANIM's lending book clean and recovers what it can when businesses fall behind.
