Why 3%

Enough to make it worth it. Not predatory.

3% equity is the sweet spot. It's enough to make the lending sustainable — ORPHANIM can trade on the spread and profit from market movement, and the 3% equity gives upside if the business succeeds. It's not predatory — the business keeps its cash flow, keeps control, just gives up 3%.

Why 0% Interest

Profit from trading, not from the borrower.

Traditional lenders charge interest because that's how they make money. ORPHANIM doesn't need to — the AI trading engine generates returns from the spread. The business gets the cash it needs without interest payments. ORPHANIM makes its money from trading, plus the 3% equity upside.

Automated trading on the spread

AI trading strategies run on the spread between what's lent and what's collected. The AI watches the market, makes trades, generates returns from market movement — not from charging interest to businesses. This is what makes 0% interest possible.
