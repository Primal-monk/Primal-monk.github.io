The idea

Banks charge 30% interest and gut you when you can't pay. We don't.

Business expansion loans from traditional banks are brutal — interest rates as high as 30%, and if you can't pay, they take your entire company and gut it. ORPHANIM does the opposite: we take a small 3% equity stake in your company, and in exchange you get an interest-free loan backed by that equity. No interest payments. No debt. Just a percentage of your company, and cash you can use to grow.

How it works

The loan is backed by your company — not by you personally.

You don't personally guarantee the loan. Your company does — through the 3% equity stake we hold. If the company succeeds, you pay back on your terms and can buy back the equity anytime. If it fails, we take 10% of the company to cover the loan, and sell off the assets to turn a profit. Either way, we're fine — and so are you for having tried to grow.

The loan mechanics

Equity taken

Loan amount
Based on valuation vs. net profit — e.g. $1M company → $100K loan

0% — always — no interest, ever, on anything
Repayment

Buy back equity
Anytime, at any time — you can buy back our 3% whenever you want
If company fails
10% of company compensates — then we sell the assets

Why we ask for so little

Traditional private equity groups ask for 10–15% of your company and go after a few big targets. We ask for 3% and go after many small ones. The 3% is the only part you can't buy back — the rest, you can always buy back if you choose. We always say yes. So you're not selling your company — you're using a small piece of it as collateral for an interest-free loan. When you pay it back, you're good. When you don't want the equity anymore, you buy it back. It's completely fair to you.

"You take a loan ensured by a percentage of your company. Pay it off and you're good. You may also choose on purpose to sell a percentage of your company for that loan — basically getting it free, as if we were buying your stock." — ORPHANIM pitch

The bigger model

We build companies. Then we sell them. That's the product.

The entire ORPHANIM team's job is to help build companies, market the founder, and manage everything. We take a business idea, execute enough to make people see potential, then sell it. The parent company is never sold — only the daughter companies. This is how we scale: build, grow, sell, repeat. Some companies go public. Some get sold to other businesses. The parent always stays 100% owned.

Step 1

Take a business idea

Anything — a game studio, a hardware company, a software product. We find the idea, or it comes to us.

Step 2

Execute enough to show potential

Build the MVP, get the first customers, make it look real. Enough that people see what it could be.

Step 3

Sell it or take it public

List it at a price with what comes with it. Others can buy it. Or take it public. The parent company never sells — only daughter companies move.
