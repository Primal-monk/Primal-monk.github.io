SERAPHIM

Aerospace Concept — Draft Stage

About this page

This is a fictional business idea, drafted for a personal portfolio of company concepts. It is not a functioning organisation. It has no products, no engineering, no funding, no employees, and no relationship to any real defense contractor. The categories below are placeholders for a future, much more detailed draft — intentionally vague, with no specifications, capabilities, or operational details of any kind.

Concept

The early idea is an aerospace company built around unmanned aerial systems for non-combat roles — the kind of work that is slow, repetitive, or hard to staff for a human crew. Nothing about targeting, weapons, or combat use is part of this concept.

At this stage that is the entire idea. There is no engineering plan, no named hardware, and no timeline. This page exists so the idea is written down, not lost — not to describe something that is being built.
