CATALYST

Small chemistry, done properly.
Concept draft — not a real company yet

20% of all gross income is pledged to charity, the same rule as every ANGELCORP concept.

Catalyst is the name for now — a placeholder, same as everything else here. The idea: small, well-made chemistry products that do one clever thing really well. Three product lines so far, each collapsed below — click one open.

Product Lines

Colour-Fire Candles
Candles

Candles that burn in real, vivid colour — not a tinted wax, an actual coloured flame, the same trick chemistry classrooms use with metal salts (strontium for red, copper for green/blue, sodium for that vivid yellow-orange). A whole rack of them side by side burning six different colours at once.

The pitch: normal candles are all the same orange flame no matter what colour the wax is. These would not be.

Snow Caps / Frost-Bites Zero°
Candy & Drinks

A candy that makes your whole mouth feel freezing cold — the same feeling as brushing your teeth and then drinking orange juice, deliberately engineered into one product instead of being an accident.

The mechanism: menthol activates the cold receptors in your mouth. A mint-flavoured mouthwash already contains menthol, which is why it feels cold even though it isn’t. Add something acidic (like citrus) and something with a little alcohol, and all three effects stack — menthol for the cold sensation, acid for the sting, alcohol for the bite. That combination is the whole product.

A second idea in the same line: a carbonated drink with helium (or another safe carbonation gas) mixed in, so your voice comes out higher or lower right after drinking it. A party trick in a can — birthdays, mostly.

Unbreakable Bubbles
Bubbles

Ordinary bubbles pop the instant anything touches them. The idea here is a much tougher bubble film — one that can be touched, bounced, even briefly held, without bursting on contact the way a normal soap bubble does.
