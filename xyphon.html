The application

PHAGEIO — biology and chemistry, AI-assisted.

Much like TYPHON is an app for offensive security — PHAGEIO is the app for biological studies. For research groups to use. Matches a real tumor's profile to candidate compounds and simulates the result, fast. Biology and chemistry, both — because chemistry is needed for biology.

Researcher: "match candidate compound X against banked lung tumor sample XY-014."
↓ tumor genomic profile + compound → PHAGEIO AI
PHAGEIO AI → simulate compound response on XY-014 organoid culture
↓ AI-modeled environment, mirrors the tissue's real behavior
Result → viability drop, resistance markers, off-target signal
↓ cross-reference medical literature
