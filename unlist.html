What It Does

YouTube has a whole layer of content that's there but not shown — unlisted videos. They don't appear in search, they don't show up on the channel's public listing, but they're still accessible if you have the link. Unlist is a Chrome extension that finds them.

Point it at any channel and it scans for every unlisted video on that channel. Search by keyword and it finds unlisted videos across YouTube that match. Every result is a direct link — one click and you're watching.

Features

Mode 01

Channel Scan

Enter any YouTube channel URL or handle. Unlist scans the entire channel and returns every unlisted video — titles, thumbnails, view counts, and direct links.

Mode 02

Keyword Search

Search across YouTube for unlisted videos matching any keyword or topic. Find hidden tutorials, drafts, teardowns, and content that isn't publicly listed.

One Click

Direct Access

Every result is a direct link. Click it and the unlisted video opens on YouTube. No login needed. No friction. Just the video.

Discover

Hidden Content

The core use case: tutorials that weren't published, drafts sitting unlisted, teardowns that never went public, community Q&As — all the content that's there but hidden from the normal YouTube experience.
