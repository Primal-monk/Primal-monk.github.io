The idea

Find hidden YouTube content.

UNLIST is a Chrome extension that finds unlisted YouTube videos — the stuff that doesn't show up in search, doesn't appear on channel pages, but exists. Creators use unlisted for drafts, WIPs, private shares, behind-the-scenes. UNLIST finds it all.

Feature 01

Scan a channel

Enter any YouTube channel URL and UNLIST scans every video on that channel — including unlisted ones that don't appear publicly. Get a full list of everything uploaded, public or hidden.

Why it matters

Unlisted doesn't mean private.

Unlisted videos don't appear in YouTube search and don't show up on a channel's public video list — but anyone with the link can watch them. Creators use unlisted for drafts, WIPs, private share links, behind-the-scenes content. UNLIST finds that content.

The name

UNLIST.
