What UNLIST does

Four ways to find hidden YouTube content.

Feature 01 · Channel Scan

Scan any channel, fully.

Enter a YouTube channel URL and UNLIST crawls every video — public and unlisted. Get the complete upload history, including videos the creator hid from their own channel page.

Feature 02 · Keyword Search

Search for hidden content.

Search across YouTube for unlisted videos matching your keywords. Content that would never appear in normal search results — drafts, WIPs, private shares — surfaced by topic.

Feature 03 · Filter & Sort

Find exactly what you want.

Sort by date, length, or upload order. Filter to show only unlisted, only public, or both. Narrow results until you find the hidden content you're actually looking for.

Feature 04 · Direct Access

Watch or export instantly.

Every result is a direct link — click to open the unlisted video immediately. Export your full results as a CSV for tracking, research, or archival. Everything you find, saved.

Why it matters

Unlisted doesn't mean private.

Unlisted videos don't appear in YouTube search and don't show on a channel's public video list — but anyone with the link can watch them. Creators use unlisted for drafts, WIPs, private shares, behind-the-scenes. UNLIST finds that content.
