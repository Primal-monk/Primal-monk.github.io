Three products. One platform.

Everything feeds into everything.

DownloadHarvester is a software platform with three distinct products that all feed into each other. The freeware ad network is like Google AdSense but for software. The bloatware middleman automates the deal between bloatware companies and freeware devs. The itch.io desktop port re-categorizes and filters games.

Why this exists

For developers. For bloatware companies.

For Freeware Developers

A third option — get paid without the bloat.

Freeware devs currently have two bad options: give their software away for nothing, or bundle bloatware themselves and deal with the contracts directly. DownloadHarvester gives them a third option — package it once, get paid per download automatically. No bloatware contracts. No work. Just sensors that pay you when people download your software.

For Bloatware Companies

Downloads without the hassle.

Bloatware companies want downloads. They want reach. They want exposure. DownloadHarvester gives them a clean way to pay for that — through the middleman — without having to contract with every freeware dev individually. They pay the service, the service pays the devs per download, everyone wins.
