The Approach

OPSEC methods. Legal. AI-assisted. Automated.

PHASE 01

Gather the surface

Start with people-search-style tools — publicly available information on the subject. Name, address history, phone numbers, known associates. The standard OSINT stack, gathered first.

PHASE 02

Scan the images

Find all images on the subject's social media and auto-scan them for metadata with AI. EXIF data, geolocation hints, device fingerprints, timestamps — everything the images reveal that isn't visible on the surface.

PHASE 03

Build the profile

AI takes all the gathered data and builds a full profile on the individual of interest. Connections, habits, locations, accounts, timeline — a structured report that puts the whole picture together.

PHASE 04

Map the network

Investigate nodes the AI suspects are related to the suspect. Follow the connections. Map the network. Each related node gets checked, and the results feed back into the profile.

PHASE 05

Find the hidden accounts

Find alt-accounts using writing-style analysis — the same method referenced in the source video, where AI sees someone's writing style and filters correlated accounts with the same style. One person, many accounts, all identified.

PHASE 06

Deliver the report

Everything comes together in a full report on the individual of interest. Structured, sourced, and ready to use. The final output of the investigation — a document that says what was found, how, and where it came from.
