Men's Grooming · Concept Draft
The idea

Makeup that doesn't look like makeup.

MARKUP is men's grooming that makes you look better without anyone knowing you're wearing it. Products that are light, invisible, and comfortable — the opposite of obvious makeup. You apply it, you forget it's there, and you look better for it. Haircolour gel in every colour including black, for guys with hairloss. Jaw and neck exercisers for facial definition. Dropshipped at first, manufactured later.

The products

Two products. One idea.

Product 01

Haircolour Gel

A haircolour gel that comes in every colour — including black. For guys with hairloss, apply it to your hair and it makes it look thicker, covering bald spots and thin areas. It's barely noticeable — dry, doesn't rub off, barely feels it. And it's good for your hair and skin. Like makeup, but made actually healthy.

The audience

Who MARKUP is for.

Who This Is For

Men who want to look better — without the obviousness.

The looksmaxxing community. Young modern men who want to improve their appearance but don't want to wear obvious makeup. Men with hairloss who want to cover thin spots without anyone noticing. Men who want better jaw definition but don't want to look like they're trying too hard. This is for the guy who wants to look better and have nobody know he's doing anything about it.

The Positioning

Makeup — but healthy. Masculine. Invisible.

Makeup usually means feminine, obvious, and unhealthy. MARKUP flips all three. It's healthy — good for your hair and skin, not damaging. It's masculine — made for men, positioned for men, sold to men. And it's invisible — light, dry, non-transferable. Nobody knows you're wearing it. You just look better.

Dropship-first

Look like a real company. Then make the products.

Both products are dropshipped at first. The real strategy is to make MARKUP look like a professional company with a clean idea and sales pitch — so the dropshipping works. Build the brand, the presentation, the website. Make it look real. Then, once the demand is proven, move to own manufacturing. This is the real way to dropship: look like a pro company first, make the product later.

Marketing

Get in front of the right audience.

TikTok and Hermes agents as marketing channels for dropship ventures. The looksmaxxing audience is on TikTok. The product sells itself — a guy with hairloss applies the gel, covers his thin spots, and looks better. That's a TikTok video. That's the marketing. Combine with Hermes agents for outreach, for community engagement, for scaling the dropship.
