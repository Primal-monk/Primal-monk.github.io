The idea

Make every idea free. Then pick one to make real each month.

The ALLSEER WIKI is a free knowledge base — a collection of companies, products, and ideas, all made freely available as clean web pages. The idea: take everything we find interesting, document it clearly, and make it free. Then, every month, we pick one of those ideas and make it real — build the product, ship it, turn the wiki page into a real company.

What's in the wiki

Every idea, repackaged cleanly.

The wiki covers companies, software, hardware, finance, security — everything in the ALLSEER ecosystem. Each entry is a clean, standalone page: what the idea is, how it works, what it would take to build. Nothing fancy — just clear documentation of everything we're thinking about.

The model

Free first. Monetize later. Pick one each month.

The wiki is free — everything in it is accessible without pay. Then, every month, we take one of the wiki entries and make it real. Repackage open-source software on GitHub as a product or app with a logo and clean website. Combine many ideas into a full application. Add MCP tools. The wiki is the backlog; the monthly "make it real" is the product rollout.

The monthly cadence

Month 1
Document the idea — clean wiki page, what it is, how it works
Month 2
Repackage existing resources — open-source software, GitHub repos, tools
Month 3
Add a logo & clean website — make it look like a real product
Month 4+
Make it real — ship the product, build the business, or keep it free

How ideas get combined

Often we can combine many open-source projects into one full application — adding MCP tools, connecting things together, giving it a single brand and a clean website. The wiki entry for that combined idea becomes the landing page for the real product. Nothing new invented — just assembled cleanly.
