The Platform

Banking for the public. Not the shareholders.

The banks are not in the business of serving the people. They are in the business of serving their shareholders. Nationalized banking changes that — the banks become public institutions, and the money they control serves the public, not private profit.

This is not about destroying banking. It is about making banking serve the public — the way a road serves the public, the way a school serves the public, the way the money system should serve the people it is made of.

"The money is not the banks' money. It is the people's money. The banks are just the pipes." Nationalized Banking Platform · Principle 1

Nationalized banking means the banks are publicly owned. Publicly accountable. Publicly directed. The profit does not go to shareholders — it goes back into the system, into the people, into the public good. Lower interest. Better service. Banks that actually serve the people they are made of.

PRINCIPLE 01

Public Ownership

The banks are owned by the public. Not private shareholders. Not foreign capital. The people, through the state, own the banks.

PRINCIPLE 02

Public Accountability

The banks answer to the people. Not to a board. Not to a market. The direction of the bank is set by public interest, not private gain.

PRINCIPLE 03

Public Service

The profit goes back into the system. Lower interest for the people. Better service. Banks that serve the people, not extract from them.

Why Now
The banks have failed the people

Why now.

The banks have failed the people. They take our money, they gamble with it, they crash, and they get bailed out — by us. Nationalized banking ends that cycle. The banks are owned by the people, serve the people, and are accountable to the people.

It is not a radical idea. It is the obvious one. The banks should serve the public — that is what public infrastructure does.

The Ideology

What we believe.

Nationalized banking is part of a larger idea — that the things the people depend on should be owned by the people, served by the people, and accountable to the people. The banks. The land. The water. The energy. The things that everybody needs, everybody owns.

Individual profit is not the goal. The goal is a society that works — a society where the money serves the people, the banks serve the people, and the public institutions actually serve the public.

This is not socialism in the old sense. It is not the state running everything. It is the people owning the things they depend on — and the banks are among the most important of those things.

"Everything that everybody needs — everybody owns. The banks are no exception." Nationalized Banking Platform · Principle 4
IDEOLOGY 01

The Public Good

The public interest is the goal. Not individual profit. Not shareholder value. The public good — a society that works for the people it is made of.

IDEOLOGY 02

Public Ownership of Essentials

The things everybody depends on should be owned by everybody. The banks, the land, the water, the energy — the essentials are public.

IDEOLOGY 03

Accountability

Power is accountable to the people. The banks are accountable to the people. No more unaccountable private power over the public's money.

Nationalized Banking · People's Manifesto
Chapter I · The Problem

The banks are in the business of private profit. They take the people's money, gamble with it, crash, and get bailed out by the people. The people's money serves the banks — not the other way around.

This is the problem. The banks are not accountable to the people. They are accountable to their shareholders. And the people's money is not being used for the people.

Chapter II · The Solution

Nationalized banking. The banks are owned by the public. Accountable to the public. Directed by the public interest. The profit goes back into the system — into the people, into the public good.

The money is the people's money. The banks are just the pipes. The pipes should serve the people.

Chapter III · The Principles

Public Ownership. The banks are owned by the people, through the state.

Public Accountability. The banks answer to the people, not to a board or a market.

Public Service. The profit goes back into the system. Lower interest. Better service. Banks that serve the people.

Chapter IV · The Larger Idea

Everything that everybody needs — everybody owns. The banks, the land, the water, the energy. The essentials are public. The public good is the goal. Individual profit is not the goal.

This is not about destroying banking. It is about making banking serve the people — the way public infrastructure serves the public.

Chapter V · The Movement

This is a movement, not a party. A platform, not a personality. The banks belong to the people — and it is time we said so, and meant it.
