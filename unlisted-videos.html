UNLISTED VIDEOS SCANNER

Discover the hidden content on YouTube.
Chrome Extension Concept · Not Live

What It Does

YouTube has a lot of unlisted videos — videos that aren't publicly listed on a channel but are still accessible if you have the link. The Unlisted Videos Scanner is a Chrome extension that scans YouTube for unlisted videos and scans channels for their unlisted videos, so you can discover and access that hidden content.

It's a simple idea with a clear use case: there's a whole layer of YouTube content that's not publicly visible but is still there. This extension gives you a window into it.

Features

Feature 01

Channel Scanning

Enter any YouTube channel URL or handle, and the extension scans that channel for all unlisted videos — presenting a list of titles, thumbnails, and direct links.

Feature 02

Keyword Search

Search for unlisted videos by keyword or topic. The extension queries across YouTube for unlisted content matching your search term.

Feature 03

Direct Access

Every result includes a direct link — click to open the unlisted video on YouTube. No login required, just the extension and the link.

Feature 04

Discover Hidden Content

The core use case: there's a whole layer of YouTube content that's not publicly visible but is still there. This gives you a window into it — tutorials, drafts, deleted-but-unlisted content, and more.
