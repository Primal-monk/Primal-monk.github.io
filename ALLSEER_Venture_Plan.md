## 3. New business ideas from NotesForHermes.txt — not yet on the site

These are ideas that need their own pages eventually but are not built yet. Each should get its own page under ALLSEER when it moves from "note" to "real concept."

### 3.1 Finance / Investment Bank concept
**Idea:** Own "investment bank" or trading group. Maximal-return focus. Possibly:
- AI that watches social media / Reddit to gauge market sentiment, makes trading decisions
- Options trading, shorting strategies, or safe long-term investing
- Debit card where your money is always invested but spendable instantly
- Or just a regular investment group: we invest your money, give maximal returns, also act as an interest-free bank
- Apps with pixel-art AI branding (Drawberry style), desktop + phone + web

**Not legal/financial advice — concept only.**  
**Suggested page slot:** Future daughter company under ALLSEER, own page.

### 3.2 Freeware "ad network" / bloatware middleman
**Idea:** Middleman service between bloatware companies and freeware developers. Auto-detects downloads, automatically pays the freeware company per download. Bloatware companies pay for the service. Page full of bloatware-distributed free software. Open-source packaging method so any freeware can plug in and get paid automatically.

**Suggested page slot:** Future daughter company. Could also be a PICODEV-product-adjacent software project.

### 3.3 YouTube unlisted-video scanner (Chrome extension)
**Idea:** Chrome extension that scans YouTube for all unlisted videos, scans channels for unlisted videos, lets you find/access them.

**Suggested page slot:** TWEETSHIELD-adjacent browser tool page, or its own small extension page.

### 3.4 Digital private investigator / OPSEC investigation service
**Idea:** Fully digital PI service. Uses OPSEC tools to legally find information on a suspect. AI filters real requests. Could offer red-team cybersecurity. AI-assisted investigation:
- PeopleSearch-style tools
- Scan all social media images, auto-extract metadata with AI
- AI builds full profile, finds related nodes, alt-accounts
- Writing-style analysis to correlate accounts (similar to the referenced YouTube method)
- Report generation

**Positioning overlap with TYPHON.** Could be a TYPHON sub-service or its own company.

**Suggested page slot:** Either a TYPHON service page or its own investigation-company page.

### 3.5 AI call center / lead-gen service
**Idea:** AI call centers for various services. Collect phone numbers, categorize by likely interest, run pay-to-buy call center. Any company can use it to sell their product. Also other advertising paths beyond call centers.

**Suggested page slot:** Future B2B services company page.

### 3.6 Bloatware-to-freeware packaging service (alternative framing of 3.2)
Same idea as 3.2, phrased as a service for freeware developers: package your freeware with our official bloatware wrapper, get paid per download automatically. No work on your end.

### 3.7 Work-PC-to-gaming-PC conversion / "open-source factory"
**Idea:** Modify work PCs into gaming PCs — mostly a new case / rebuild. Shipped assembled. Secondary business. Could partner with furniture store chains. Sold on Amazon. "Open-source factory" model — people put it together, shipped finished.

### 3.8 Multi-business / warehouse-sharing organization
**Idea:** If you're a store with low activity, give us access to your warehouse for dropshipping or other products. We're the middleman that organizes multi-business logistics.

### 3.9 Tax consultation / accounting company (AI-assisted)
**Idea:** Use QuickBooks and Intuit tools + AI to do company accounting and taxes. Companies pay for the service. Can also offer law services. Same model as debt collection (3.10).

### 3.10 Debt collection agency (AI-call-based)
**Idea:** AI calls people with debt obligations, applies pressure tactics, tries to get them to pay. Offer the service to banks and finance companies. Option to buy debt at discount and collect. Percentage-based contracts.

### 3.11 Parent-company / tax-free LLC setup service
**Idea:** File companies under us to get them tax-free via offshore or loophole structures. Loan-company-money model secured by company percentage. Max tax-free loopholes available to everyone. Call-based sales.

### 3.12 Private equity / business-funding company
**Idea:** Take ~3% of a company in exchange for interest-free loans backed by company equity. Much lower take than traditional PE (10-15%). Loan amount based on company valuation vs net profit equation. If loan not repaid, 10% of company compensates. Company can buy back equity anytime. If company fails, gut and sell assets (PE standard play).

**This is the "make companies" model** — the meta-business. The entire team's job is to help build companies, market the founder, manage things. Sell daughter companies down the line. Parent company never sold.

**Suggested page slot:** This is big enough to be a top-level ALLSEER division once real — maybe "ALLSEER VENTURES" or "ALLSEER CAPITAL."

### 3.13 RC toy / robot redesign company
**Idea:** Turn hobbyist designs into RC toys, redesign toys into "robots," sell under a robot company. Some drop-shipped, some re-characterized.

**Suggested page slot:** Could fold into PICODEV-adjacent hardware, or its own consumer-products page.

### 3.14 Forum website (TYPHON / OSIRIS hacking forum)
**Idea:** Forum for TYPHON (or OSIRIS). People modify and share things. Accepts monero / bitcoin. Concept-for-now but build it anyway.

**Suggested page slot:** TYPHON sub-page (forum) or its own community page.

## 4. Cross-cutting infrastructure (not a company, but runs across all)

### 4.1 Dropship-first strategy (applied to any consumer product company)
**Rule:** Any consumer-product idea (MARKUP, future grooming, future physical goods) starts drop-shipped. The page must look indistinguishable from a real product company — professional design, clean pitch, real-feeling product photography (or placeholder that reads as real). Swap in real inventory / custom manufacturing later. This is the go-to-market, not a compromise.

**Applies to:** MARKUP now. Anything with a physical SKU later.

### 4.2 TikTok + Hermes-agent marketing pipeline
**Idea:** Use short-form video (TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Reels) to market dropship products. Hermes agents can:
- Script videos
- Generate posting schedules
- Write captions / hashtags
- A/B test hooks

**Applies to:** MARKUP first. Any consumer product with a visual demo later.

### 4.3 Charity pledge (mandatory for every ALLSEER company)
**Rule:** Every company page under ALLSEER carries the 20% charity pledge. This is non-negotiable branding. Already implemented on omni.html, catalyst.html, and all venture pages.

### 4.4 "Concept draft" labeling
**Rule:** Any page that is not a real, shipping product must be clearly labeled "Concept draft — not a real company yet" or similar. Already standard across all venture pages. No pretending.
