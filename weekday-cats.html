The Story of Weekday Cats.

Weekday Cats started with a simple idea: one cat for each weekday. Monday cat, Tuesday cat, Wednesday cat, Thursday cat, Friday cat, Saturday cat, Sunday cat — seven cats, seven days, all plushies, all just cats.

It's not a big idea. It doesn't have to be. The cats are cute. People like cute cat stuff. Put the cats on plushies, put the cats on merch, put the cats on Printify and Redbubble — and that's Weekday Cats.

Just cats. That's the whole thing.
